followed properly and reducing the likelihood of human error.

Binary Search:
It is a highly efficient search algorithm that works by repeatedly dividing the
search interval in half and comparing the middle element to the target element. If the
target is equal to the middle element, the search is successful. If the target is smaller,
the search continues on the left half of the list; if larger, it continues on the right half.
This process is repeated until the target element is found or it is determined that the
element does not exist in the list.
