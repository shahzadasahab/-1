Many businesses begin with a simple problem. A student forgets homework. A
shopkeeper cannot show new designs to customers. A college van arrives late and

6.5
Types of Prototypes:
Prototypes are commonly divided into three types according to the level of
detail. These are low-fidelity, mid- fidelity, and high-fidelity prototypes. The word
fidelity means how close the prototype is to the final product. A low-fidelity prototype
is simple and fog while a high-fidelity prototype looks more like the final product.
6.5.1 Low-Fidelity Prototype:
A low-fidelity prototype is a quick and rough model. It is ssually made at the
very beginning of an idea. It may be drawn on paper or chart paper. It does not focus
on colors, fonts, or final design. Its purpose is to show the main idea and user flow.
This type is very. suitable for college students because it is cheap, fast, and easy to
change.
6.5.2 Mid-Fidelity Prototype:
- A mid-fidelity prototype is clearer than a paper sketch.
It may be aieated
| in
PowerPoint, Canva, Figma, or another digital tool. It shows screens and navigation in
4 more organized way, but it may not contain final graphics or real data, It is useful
when students want to test whether users can move from one screen to another without
confusion,
6.5.3 High-Fidelity Prototype:
A high-fidelity prototype looks close to the final anndlaet
It may include
realistic. colors, buttons, sample data, and some working features, It takes more time
than low-fidelity and mid-fidelity prototypes. Therefore, students should normally
create it only after the basic idea has already been tested.

product, It includes only the most important features needed to test the idea with real
users. The word minimum means it has only essential features. The word viable means
it 1s usable enough to solve the main problem,
_
An MVP is not the final product. It is also not only
a drawing. It is a small
working version used to test if users actually n need the solution. For example, a complete
canteen app may include login, payment, delivery tracking, ratings, and discount offers.
But the MVP may only be a Google Form where students select snacks before break:
If students'use the form, then the team has evidence that the idea may be useful.
