1.4 Importance of HCI
Human-Computer Interaction (HCI)
is important because
it ensures
that
machines are approachable and usable, and it turns complex technology into everyday
tools for people. Good design makes technology simple and enjoyable, for example
smartphones with touchscreens allow easy access and online learning apps guide
students clearly. On the other hand, poor design can cause confusion and risks, for
example unclear buttons on medical devices may lead to errors and crowded websites
with too many ads frustrate users. Well-designed interfaces help people finish tasks
faster and with fewer mistakes. This improves efficiency and makes work easier.
Following are the important implications of HCI.

'

on mobile browsers.

so people can use it easily. Good systems simplify actions and reduce extra steps, give

¢
Ensure Consistency refers to Keep layouts, icons, and commands uniform
across the system. Example; Using the same “Save” button design everywhere.
©
Provide Clear Feedback refers to Show users what happened after an action.
Example: A message saying “Payment Successful” after checkout.
