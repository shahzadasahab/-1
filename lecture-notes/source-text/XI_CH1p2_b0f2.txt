Phase 5: Deployment (May)

Pilot Release strategy was used: the librarian used the system alone for two weeks
before it was opened to students. This allowed any remaining issues to be identified in
a controlled environment.

Project Outcome:
The Al-Noor Library Management System was delivered on schedule and
within the agreed budget. The following results were recorded after the first term of
operation:
e
The system was completed in exactly 6 months as planned, with no schedule
overrun.
e
All 2,400 library books were entered into the database within the first week of
deployment.
e
Students can now locate any available book in under five seconds using the
search feature.
e
Overdue book returns decreased by 70% in the first term compared to the paper-
based system.
e
The librarian estimated a saving of approximately three hours of administrative
work per week.
Key Observation:
The Waterfall model worked well in this project because the
requirements were completely defined before development began and did not change
during the six-month period. The model's rigid structure kept the team focused and the
project on schedule.
