digital artefact. (Suggested duration: 3 weeks (9-10 periods))

Differentiate qualitative vs quantitative data, and primary vs secondary data.

Design a suitable data-collection approach (interview, survey, prototype, observation, or
simulation) for a research question.
¢
Apply best practices to present primary and secondary data for a research question.
